# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PharmaML is an open research-software community. We are committed to a welcoming, safe and constructive environment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity or orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and considerate in technical discussions.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project conventions without condescension.
- Give actionable, specific feedback about code and ideas rather than people.
- Accept correction, respect differing viewpoints and acknowledge mistakes.
- Protect privacy, confidential research and other people's data.
- Credit prior work and represent validation results honestly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Sexualized attention or unwelcome sexual content.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption or bad-faith review manipulation.
- Submitting malicious code, credentials, private data or deliberately misleading scientific claims.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional research setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, releases and other project spaces, and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

Maintainers may remove or reject comments, commits, code and other contributions that violate this policy. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented and consistent.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner using the contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Include relevant links and context, but do not repost sensitive material publicly. The maintainer will respect the reporter's privacy as far as practical while reviewing the report.

Project maintainers are also subject to this policy.
